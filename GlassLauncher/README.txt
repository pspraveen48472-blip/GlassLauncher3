BUILD: Install Android Studio > File > Open > GlassLauncher > let Gradle sync > Build > Build APK(s).
APK: app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
INSTALL: copy to a USB stick, or "adb connect <tv-ip>:5555" then "adb install app-debug.apk".
SET AS HOME: press Home on the remote and choose Glass Launcher > Always.
CUSTOMIZE: LiquidBackground.kt (glow colors) and GlassDrawable.kt (glass strength).
CUSTOMIZE SCREEN: pick the "Customize" card at the end of the app row, or press the MENU key on your remote.
HDMI: HDMI/AV chips appear above the dock. They depend on your TV exposing its inputs to apps; if none show, your TV hides them.
TOP BAR: glass bar at top right with Wi-Fi, Customize (launcher settings) and System (Android settings). Hide it in Customize > Top bar.
TOP BAR: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, system Settings and Customize buttons at the top right. Wi-Fi/Bluetooth icons dim when off.
REAL GLASS: every panel shows a blurred, magnified copy of your wallpaper. Pick a wallpaper (or your own image) in Customize.
FAVORITES: long-press OK on an app > Add to favorites / App info / Uninstall. Favorites move to the front with a star.
